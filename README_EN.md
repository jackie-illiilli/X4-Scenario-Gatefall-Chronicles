# Gatefall Chronicles

One-line summary: Gatefall Chronicles is an original hidden Timelines campaign for X4 about anomalous Xenon jump technology, the collapse of the gate network, and rival factions forced from mistrust and civil war into a desperate coalition counteroffensive.

The current public build now contains **eight complete, scored scenarios**:

- `Gatefall 01: Under the Surface`
- `Gatefall 02: Passing the Buck`
- `Gatefall 03: Fight for Survival`
- `Gatefall 04: In the Same Boat`
- `Gatefall 05: Judgement Day`
- `Gatefall 06: Lifeline`
- `Gatefall 07: Forlorn Hope`
- `Gatefall 08: Hamartia`

Chinese version: [README.md](README.md)

## Latest Update: Gatefall 08 — Hamartia

This update completes `Gatefall 08: Hamartia`. With the final scenario released, the eight-scenario main campaign of Gatefall Chronicles is now complete.

The finale begins in the strategic opening created by `Alternative XIV`. You play as Siegfried, captain of the Asgard-class battleship `Paradox`, leading the surviving coalition forces from Matrix #79 towards the Xenon jump installation in Savage Spur I.

The operation includes:

- Commanding three simultaneous strike groups against Xenon power stations to collapse the defensive energy network.
- Deploying a Tactical Production Platform and returning to the shipbuilding and resource-management mechanics introduced in Scenario 04.
- Destroying Xenon ship production and a superweapon capable of jumping antimatter bombs directly into the coalition formation.
- Surviving a strategic Xenon ambush triggered by a hidden jump beacon, leaving `Paradox` badly damaged and forcing the offensive to fall back.
- Recharging `Paradox` first at the production platform and then around the wreckage of a destroyed Xenon power station after the forward base is abandoned.
- Fighting renewed Xenon production and multiple rebuilt superweapons while using improvised production space inside the damaged `Paradox` to assemble new frigates and strike craft.
- Launching a second assault on the gate defence, then splitting the coalition: Delta Flight, the 86th Escort Fleet, Boron aviation, Split and other surviving forces hold Matrix #79 while Terran destroyers follow `Paradox` into Savage Spur I.
- Carrying the operation through to the Xenon jump installation and ending the Gatefall.

The English title is standardised as `Gatefall 08: Hamartia`, directly continuing the tribute to `Mental Omega 3.3.6` through Allied missions `Withershins / Hamartia`, Epsilon finale `Babel`, Siegfried, and the Paradox Engine. Mission 08's L044 text has also received a dedicated localisation and tribute pass.

## Campaign Saving

X4: Timelines does not natively provide mid-scenario saving. Gatefall Chronicles implements its own in-scenario save/load system.

Loading a campaign save also restores the BGM assigned to the current phase, preventing combat or story music from being lost after reloading.

## Installation

If you downloaded the project from GitHub:

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Copy the entire `extensions/gatefall_chronicles` folder into your X4 `extensions` directory.
3. Start X4 and confirm that `Gatefall Chronicles` is enabled in the Extensions menu.

Typical folder structure:

```text
X4 Foundations/
└─ extensions/
   └─ gatefall_chronicles/
```

## How to Start the Campaign

This is not a normal sandbox mission chain. It is accessed through the hidden Timelines scenario interface.

1. Launch the game and enter `Timelines`.
2. Enter the Timelines Hub, the black void between scenarios.
3. Find the hidden campaign / hidden scenario access point.
4. Open the hidden scenario list.
5. Scroll to the end of the list and find `Gatefall Chronicles`.
6. Select the scenario you want to play.

If you have already accessed the official hidden scenarios, Gatefall Chronicles uses the same scenario list.

## Current Public Content

### Scenario 01: Under the Surface

Status: Complete and scored

- Deploy a long-range reconnaissance satellite in the Mists of Artemis with a Terran wingman.
- Investigate a destroyed Kha'ak hive and an anomalous spatial-fold signal.
- Get dragged into the Xenon core territory of Savage Spur and break out towards a Terran outpost.
- Hold until the Terran Intervention Corps battleship `Paragon` arrives.

Key features: frigate combat, capital-ship engine attacks, outpost defence, multi-stage cutscenes, and scored results.

### Scenario 02: Passing the Buck

Status: Complete and scored

- Play as an Argon rear commander directing HAT's 86th Escort Fleet from Hatikvah's Choice.
- Use OOS map command to handle overlapping attacks on civilian and strategic targets.
- Infiltrate a classified database and obtain a Yaki navigation protocol capable of changing Xenon target priorities.
- When Argon chooses to sacrifice the 86th, redirect the Xenon capital offensive into ZYA territory to save Rayna, Shin, and the fleet.

Key features: station-interior sequences, OOS command, multi-front defence, database infiltration, navigation-beacon deployment, and scoring.

### Scenario 03: Fight for Survival

Status: Complete and scored

- Return to the ruins of Family Phi aboard a failing Split ship and take command of the Rattlesnake `Phi Six`.
- Reunite with the Phi squad and learn that your parents are aboard the refugee carrier `Autumn of Phi`.
- Escort the convoy across the northern Split front under recurring Xenon capital-ship attacks.
- Survive the Kha'ak Annihilator `Long Night`, rescue civilians, and turn an abandoned factory into the final trap.

Key features: Rattlesnake combat, refugee escort, squad command, recurring capital reinforcements, dynamic music, explicit failure conditions, and five-star scoring.

### Scenario 04: In the Same Boat

Status: Complete and scored

- Protect a salvage operation and recover blockade intelligence.
- Lead a Boron–Terran fleet against Xenon radar ships and long-range fire support.
- Deploy a player-controlled Tactical Production Platform and build a fleet from renewable resources.
- Conduct an RTS-style offensive against Xenon power, shipbuilding, and the M0 itself.

Key features: large fleet battles, player shipbuilding, renewable resources, secondary objectives, recurring reinforcements, cutscenes, and five-star scoring.

### Scenario 05: Judgement Day

Status: Complete and scored

- Infiltrate Argon space from the perspective of the Second Terraformer Fleet / Xenon.
- Compromise communications and strategic power infrastructure, then deploy jump beacons.
- Command a Xenon CPU Ship and assemble fleets through mission orders in a pseudo-RTS production system.
- Break Argon Prime's engineering, industrial, and political infrastructure, then intercept the returning First Expedition.
- Witness the wider Gatefall as rival Commonwealth powers are struck while still divided by mistrust and war.

Key features: infiltration, pseudo-RTS production, CPU Ship command, large station assaults, gate interdiction, multi-sector presentation, and scoring.

### Scenario 06: Lifeline

Status: Complete and scored

- Play as Paranid commander Domel and rebuild the Flashpoint defence around `Domel III` and four hastily assembled Zeus carriers.
- Command Delta Flight's VF-31 interceptors and VF-29 torpedo craft while escorting TRI, TEL, and Split evacuation convoys.
- Reunite with Rayna and the 86th Escort Fleet, rescue crippled civilian ships, and transfer evacuees between surviving vessels.
- Launch a four-flight decapitation strike against a Xenon CPU Ship assembling reinforcements.
- Abandon Flashpoint only after `Phi Six`, `Autumn of Phi`, and the final Split convoy are on the road to Getsu Fune.

Key features: carrier aviation, multi-faction refugee escorts, emergency repair and personnel transfer, CPU Ship decapitation, dynamic custom music, and five-star scoring.

### Scenario 07: Forlorn Hope

Status: Complete and scored

- Begin exactly where `Lifeline` ends: command the high-mobility Asgard `Paragon` and rescue the exhausted coalition evacuation force at Getsu Fune.
- Develop `Alternative XIV` with Sumika, the Terrans, PIO, and coalition intelligence while Siegfried and Domel lead the main fleet towards Savage Spur I.
- Seize and hold a Xenon large communications terminal, pulling increasing numbers of defenders into Savage Spur II.
- Survive a CPU Ship, Annihilators, Ravagers, and repeated jump reinforcements as the operation expands beyond its original scope.
- When `Paragon` loses its engines, escape through Falx → Gladius → spacesuit while closing the gate and carrying Sumika / 00-Unit through Takeru's neural interface.

Key features: high-mobility Asgard combat, direct continuity from Scenario 06, strategic lure mechanics, recurring Xenon reinforcements, communications-terminal defence, chained vehicle escape, dynamic visual impairment, and full results.

### Scenario 08: Hamartia

Status: Complete and scored

- Play as Siegfried aboard `Paradox` and lead the final coalition offensive from Matrix #79 into Savage Spur I.
- Split the coalition into three strike groups and destroy the Xenon power stations feeding the core defensive network.
- Deploy a Tactical Production Platform, rebuild your fleet, and race to destroy Xenon ship production plus an antimatter jump superweapon.
- Survive a hidden-beacon ambush that cripples `Paradox`, then fight a retreat while recharging its reserve energy.
- Abandon the production platform and use energy cells scattered around a destroyed Xenon power station to keep the operation alive.
- Fight renewed Xenon production and superweapons while assembling frigates and strike craft from improvised facilities inside `Paradox`.
- Break the gate defence on a second assault, leave Delta Flight, the 86th, Boron and Split forces to hold Matrix #79, and take the Terran destroyer force into Savage Spur I.
- When the main gun is finally lost, execute the last contingency and destroy the Xenon jump installation.

Key features: three-front fleet command, Tactical Production Platform, timed superweapons, jump ambushes, retreat and recharging, improvised ship production, a coalition-wide final offensive, capital-ship endgame, and full scoring.

## Notes

- The mod uses the hidden Timelines scenario interface and does not replace the official Timelines storyline.

## Tribute

`Gatefall 02: Passing the Buck` continues a tribute to `86—Eighty-Six` through Rayna, Shin, and the 86th Escort Fleet.

`Gatefall 03: Fight for Survival` pays tribute to `Halo: Reach` and `Halo: Combat Evolved` through character names, ship names, story presentation, and music.

`Gatefall 04: In the Same Boat` is structurally inspired by the fifth Allied mission of `Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3`, `North Sea: The Unfathomable Fortress`.

`Gatefall 05: Judgement Day` pays tribute to Epsilon Mission 13, `The Conqueror`, from `Mental Omega 3.3.6`.

`Gatefall 06: Lifeline` is a broad tribute to carrier aviation and evacuation stories:

- Domel, `Domel III`, and the carriers Balgray, Lamvea, Darold, and Schderg reference the Battle of the Rainbow Star Cluster in `Space Battleship Yamato 2199`.
- Delta Flight, the VF-31 / VF-29 designations, and the names Hayate, Mirage, Arad, and Messer reference `Macross Delta`; the CPU Ship decapitation sequence also draws on `Macross Frontier`.
- Rayna, Shin, and the 86th continue the `86—Eighty-Six` tribute, while the ANT flagship `Sidonia` references `Knights of Sidonia`.

`Gatefall 07: Forlorn Hope` takes its English title and `Paragon` motif from Starsector's `Forlorn Hope`. Takeru Shirogane, Sumika / 00-Unit, Alternative XIV, and much of the character dynamic and imagery are tributes to `Muv-Luv Alternative` and related works.

`Gatefall 08: Hamartia` is a direct structural tribute to the Allied finales `Withershins` and `Hamartia` and the Epsilon finale `Babel` from `Mental Omega 3.3.6`. Siegfried, `Paradox`, the progression from an apparently successful offensive into repeated retreats and recharging, and the final use of the ship itself all deliberately echo Siegfried and the Paradox Engine.
