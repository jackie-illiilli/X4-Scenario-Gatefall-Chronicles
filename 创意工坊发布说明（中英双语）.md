- 加入 CPU 母舰、湮灭者、蹂躏者和循环跃迁增援，以及“典范号→镰刀→短剑→宇航服”的连续逃生。

这是一个基于《X4: Timelines》的原创隐藏时间线战役。Xenon获得异常跃迁能力后，星门共同体在猜忌与内战中被逐步击穿；幸存阵营必须学会真正联合，才能从撤离走向反攻。

[h2]当前公开战役[/h2]
[b]01 暗流涌动[/b]：侦察异常跃迁、逃离野蛮疾驰并守住TER前哨站。
[b]02 祸水东引[/b]：OOS指挥第86护卫队，并利用Yaki导航协议将Xenon主力引向ZYA。
[b]03 逃出生天[/b]：驾驶“菲氏6号”护送Split难民船团穿越北部战区。
[b]04 同舟共济[/b]：BOR与TER联合作战，部署战术生产平台并摧毁Xenon M0。
[b]05 审判日[/b]：从Xenon视角渗透Argon之都，以CPU母舰和伪RTS生产发动“星门陨落”。
[b]06 生命线[/b]：指挥四艘Paranid航母与Delta Flight守卫闪点，护送TRI、TEL、Split难民撤向太阳系。
[b]07 渺茫的希望[/b]：驾驶“典范号”救援月之舟、执行Alternative XIV，并在关闭星门前从野蛮疾驰II生还。

[h2]主要玩法[/h2]
- 中英文字幕、多阶段目标与分镜
- OOS远程指挥、难民护送、航母航空兵与大规模舰队战

- Mod自实现的战役保存/读档，读档后恢复当前Phase BGM

3. 在列表末尾找到 Gatefall Chronicles。
4. 选择希望游玩的关卡。

## English Workshop Description

```text
[h1]Gatefall Chronicles — An Original X4 Timelines Campaign[/h1]

[h2]Latest Update: Gatefall 07 — Forlorn Hope + English Localisation Refresh[/h2]

- Completed Gatefall 07: Forlorn Hope, opening directly where Lifeline ends at Getsu Fune.
- Command Takeru Shirogane's high-mobility Asgard, Paragon, alongside Siegfried's Paradox to relieve Domel, Delta Flight, the 86th Escort Fleet, and the evacuation force.
- Added Alternative XIV: seize a Xenon large communications terminal and lure defenders from Savage Spur I—and later jump reinforcements from beyond the gate network—into Savage Spur II.
- Added a CPU Ship, Annihilators, Ravagers, recurring jump waves, and the chained Paragon → Falx → Gladius → spacesuit escape.
- Completed Chinese/English subtitles, cutscenes, dynamic visual impairment, failure handling, scoring, campaign saving, and phase-BGM restoration.
- Comprehensively revised the English L044 text across Scenarios 01–07 and standardised intended tribute terms such as Forlorn Hope, Paragon, Delta Flight, Lamvea, and Alternative XIV.

[h2]About[/h2]
Gatefall Chronicles is an original hidden Timelines campaign for X4. After the Xenon acquire anomalous jump capability, the gate community collapses under mistrust and civil war. The survivors must learn to cooperate, first to evacuate—and eventually to counterattack.

[h2]Released Scenarios[/h2]
[b]01 Under the Surface[/b] — Investigate the anomalous jump signal, break out of Savage Spur, and defend a Terran outpost.
[b]02 Passing the Buck[/b] — Command the 86th Escort Fleet OOS and use a Yaki navigation protocol to redirect the Xenon into ZYA territory.
[b]03 Fight for Survival[/b] — Take command of Phi Six and escort a Split refugee convoy across the northern front.
[b]04 In the Same Boat[/b] — Fight with a Boron–Terran coalition, deploy a Tactical Production Platform, and destroy the Xenon M0.
[b]05 Judgement Day[/b] — Infiltrate Argon Prime from a Xenon perspective, use CPU-Ship pseudo-RTS production, and trigger the Gatefall.
[b]06 Lifeline[/b] — Command four Paranid carriers and Delta Flight while escorting TRI, TEL, and Split refugees towards the Solar System.
[b]07 Forlorn Hope[/b] — Rescue the Getsu Fune convoy aboard Paragon, execute Alternative XIV, and escape Savage Spur II while shutting the gate behind you.

[h2]Features[/h2]
- Chinese and English subtitles, scripted cutscenes, and multi-stage objectives
- OOS command, refugee escorts, carrier aviation, and large fleet battles
- Player shipbuilding / mission-driven production, renewable resources, and recurring enemy waves
- Custom BGM, explicit failure conditions, detailed scoring, and star ratings
- In-scenario save/load implemented by the mod, with phase BGM restored after loading

[h2]How to Access[/h2]
Gatefall Chronicles uses the hidden Timelines scenario interface:
1. Enter the Timelines Hub.
2. Open the hidden campaign / hidden scenario list.
3. Scroll to the end and find Gatefall Chronicles.
4. Select the scenario you want to play.

[h2]Progress[/h2]
Scenarios 01–07: complete, independently playable, and scored
Scenario 08 — Crime and Punishment: concept stage

[h2]Requirements / Notes[/h2]
- Requires X4: Foundations, Timelines, Cradle of Humanity, Split Vendetta, and Kingdom End.
- Presentation currently uses Chinese and English subtitles; there is no voice acting.
- The mod does not replace or interfere with the official Timelines storyline.
- Later scenarios, presentation, art, balance, and localisation remain in development.

[h2]Tributes[/h2]
Gatefall Chronicles uses names, scenario structures, presentation, and music as non-commercial tributes to several works:
- 02: 86—Eighty-Six
- 03: Halo: Reach / Halo: Combat Evolved
- 04: Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3
- 05: Mental Omega 3.3.6 — The Conqueror
- 06: Space Battleship Yamato 2199, Macross Delta / Frontier, 86—Eighty-Six, Knights of Sidonia
- 07: Starsector — Forlorn Hope; Muv-Luv Alternative

The four carriers in Lifeline use the names Balgray, Lamvea, Darold, and Schderg. Scenario 07 uses the English title Forlorn Hope and the battleship name Paragon.

All referenced names, music, and original works remain the property of their respective creators and rights holders. Gatefall Chronicles is a non-commercial fan project and is not officially affiliated with any of them.

For copyright concerns involving BGM or other material, please leave a Workshop comment or open an Issue at:
[url=https://github.com/jackie-illiilli/X4-Scenario-Gatefall-Chronicles]GitHub / Gatefall Chronicles[/url]
```
