```text
《星门陨落08：罪与罚》现已完成。至此，Gatefall Chronicles 从《暗流涌动》到《罪与罚》的八关主线全部可以完整游玩并结算。

最终关中，你将扮演“悖论号”舰长西格弗里德，率领星门网络最后的联合舰队从矩阵 #79 向野蛮疾驰 I 发起总攻：
- 三线指挥 86 护卫队、Delta Flight、BOR / TER / ANT / Split 联军摧毁 Xenon 能源网络
- 部署战术生产平台，生产战机、护卫舰与驱逐舰
- 限时摧毁 Xenon 造船设施与能够跃迁反物质炸弹的超级武器
- 遭遇隐藏跃迁信标伏击后重整、撤退并为“悖论号”重新充能
- 在 Xenon 重建生产设施后利用“悖论号”内部改造区继续生产增援
- 发动第二次星门攻势，最终进入野蛮疾驰 I 摧毁 Xenon 跃迁中枢

任务8英文标题为 [b]Gatefall 08: Hamartia[/b]，并完成对应 L044 英文本地化与致敬用语校对。

[h2]简介[/h2]
这是一个基于《X4: Timelines》的原创隐藏时间线战役。Xenon获得异常跃迁能力后，星门共同体在猜忌与内战中被逐步击穿；幸存阵营必须从各自为战走向真正联合，并最终对Xenon核心发动反攻。

[h2]完整八关[/h2]
[b]01 暗流涌动[/b]：侦察异常跃迁、逃离野蛮疾驰并守住TER前哨站。
[b]02 祸水东引[/b]：OOS指挥第86护卫队，用Yaki导航协议把Xenon主力引向ZYA。
[b]03 逃出生天[/b]：驾驶“菲氏6号”护送Split难民船团穿越北部战区。
[b]04 同舟共济[/b]：BOR–TER联合攻势、战术生产平台与Xenon M0。
[b]05 审判日[/b]：从Xenon视角潜入Argon之都，以CPU母舰和伪RTS生产发动“星门陨落”。
[b]06 生命线[/b]：四艘Paranid航母、Delta Flight与多阵营难民撤离。
[b]07 渺茫的希望[/b]：驾驶“典范号”执行Alternative XIV，在野蛮疾驰II诱走Xenon主力并关闭星门。
[b]08 罪与罚[/b]：驾驶“悖论号”率联合舰队完成对Xenon跃迁中枢的最终进攻。

[h2]主要玩法[/h2]
- 中英文字幕、多阶段任务与连续分镜
- OOS远程指挥、难民护送、航母航空兵和大规模舰队战
- 玩家造船 / 任务式生产、动态资源与持续敌军增援
```

## English Workshop Description

```text
[h1]Gatefall Chronicles — An Original X4 Timelines Campaign[/h1]

[h2]Final Main-Campaign Update: All Eight Scenarios Complete[/h2]

Gatefall 08: Hamartia is now complete. The full eight-scenario Gatefall Chronicles main campaign can now be played from beginning to end with scored results.

In the finale, you play as Siegfried aboard the Asgard-class battleship Paradox and lead the surviving gate-network coalition from Matrix #79 towards Savage Spur I:
- Command three simultaneous fronts using the 86th Escort Fleet, Delta Flight, and Boron / Terran / ANT / Split forces
- Collapse the Xenon energy network and deploy a Tactical Production Platform
- Build fighters, frigates, and destroyers while racing to destroy Xenon ship production and an antimatter jump superweapon
- Survive a hidden-beacon jump ambush, retreat, and recharge Paradox under constant pressure
- Continue production from improvised facilities inside the damaged Paradox after the forward base is lost
- Launch a second gate assault and carry the final offensive into Savage Spur I against the Xenon jump installation

Scenario 08 uses the English title [b]Gatefall 08: Hamartia[/b], with a dedicated L044 localisation and tribute pass.

[h2]About[/h2]
Gatefall Chronicles is an original hidden Timelines campaign for X4. After the Xenon acquire anomalous jump capability, the gate community collapses under mistrust and civil war. The survivors must learn to cooperate, first to evacuate—and finally to counterattack.

[h2]All Eight Scenarios[/h2]
[b]01 Under the Surface[/b] — Reconnaissance, anomalous jump, Savage Spur breakout, Terran outpost defence.
[b]02 Passing the Buck[/b] — OOS command of the 86th and a Yaki protocol used to redirect the Xenon into ZYA territory.
[b]03 Fight for Survival[/b] — Command Phi Six and escort a Split refugee convoy across the northern front.
[b]04 In the Same Boat[/b] — Boron–Terran offensive, Tactical Production Platform, and Xenon M0.
[b]05 Judgement Day[/b] — Xenon infiltration of Argon Prime, CPU-Ship pseudo-RTS production, and the Gatefall.
[b]06 Lifeline[/b] — Four Paranid carriers, Delta Flight, and multi-faction refugee evacuation.
[b]07 Forlorn Hope[/b] — Command Paragon, execute Alternative XIV, lure the Xenon into Savage Spur II, and close the gate.
[b]08 Hamartia[/b] — Command Paradox and lead the coalition's final assault on the Xenon jump installation.

[h2]Features[/h2]
- Chinese and English subtitles, scripted cutscenes, and multi-stage objectives
- OOS command, refugee escort, carrier aviation, and large fleet battles
- Player shipbuilding / mission-driven production, renewable resources, and recurring enemy waves
- Xenon jump mechanics, CPU Ships, superweapons, and multi-sector strategic set pieces
- Custom BGM, explicit failure conditions, detailed scoring, and star ratings
- In-scenario save/load implemented by the mod, including phase-BGM restoration

[h2]How to Access[/h2]
1. Enter the Timelines Hub.
2. Open the hidden campaign / hidden scenario list.
3. Scroll to the end and find Gatefall Chronicles.
4. Select any scenario.

[h2]Requirements / Notes[/h2]
- Requires X4: Foundations, Timelines, Cradle of Humanity, Split Vendetta, and Kingdom End.
- All eight scenarios are complete, independently playable, and scored.
- Presentation currently uses Chinese and English subtitles; there is no voice acting.
- The mod does not replace or interfere with the official Timelines storyline.

[h2]Tributes[/h2]
- 02: 86—Eighty-Six
- 03: Halo: Reach / Halo: Combat Evolved
- 04: Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3
- 05: Mental Omega 3.3.6 — The Conqueror
- 06: Space Battleship Yamato 2199, Macross Delta / Frontier, 86—Eighty-Six, Knights of Sidonia
- 07: Starsector — Forlorn Hope; Muv-Luv Alternative
- 08: Mental Omega 3.3.6 — Withershins / Hamartia / Babel

Scenario 08's Siegfried, Paradox, and its progression from a successful offensive into repeated retreats, recharging, and the final contingency directly echo Siegfried and the Paradox Engine.

All referenced names, music, and original works remain the property of their respective creators and rights holders. Gatefall Chronicles is a non-commercial fan project and is not officially affiliated with any of them.
```
